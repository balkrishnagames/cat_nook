Delete Account Info

Effective 26 October 2026  ·  Cat Nook: Logic Puzzle (com.balkrishna.catnook)  ·  Balkrishna Enterprise

Cat Nook: Logic Puzzle has no accounts

There is no sign-up, no login and no profile. We hold no server-side record of you, so there is no account for us to delete. Everything the Game knows about you is stored on your own device, and you control it completely.

Google requires every app to publish a data deletion route, including apps that store nothing remotely. This page is ours.

Option 1 — delete the data, keep the Game

  1. Open Settings on your Android device.
  2. Go to Apps → Cat Nook: Logic Puzzle.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data).

This erases your level progress, fish balance, settings and statistics immediately and permanently. The Game restarts from level 1.

Option 2 — delete everything

Uninstall the Game. Android removes its entire data directory with it. Nothing is left behind and nothing is retained by us.

Data held by our partners

Advertising and crash data sits with Google, not with us. To deal with that:

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Manage or delete activity on your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Request deletion by email

If you would prefer us to handle it, or you want written confirmation, email balkrishnagames@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.

Tell us your device model and roughly when you installed the Game. We will confirm within 7 days and complete any action within 30 days. We will not ask you for personal information in order to process the request.

What we can delete: any support correspondence you have sent us.
What we cannot delete: data on your device (only you can clear that) and records Google must retain for tax or fraud purposes.
