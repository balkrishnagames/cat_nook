Cat Nook: Logic Puzzle

A cosy logic puzzle, without the nonsense

Place one cat in every row, column and nook. Every board is solvable by pure logic — never by guessing.

Solvable by logicEvery board is verified by a solver before you ever see it. There is always a next step you can reason out. You will never be asked to guess.
An ad every third levelNever in the middle of a puzzle, and never when you restart. Most puzzle games interrupt you far more often. We decided not to.
Undo and notes, freeMarking a cell as "maybe" is a core part of solving, not a premium feature. It is free, forever, and it always will be.
Plays offlinePuzzles are generated on your device. No account, no sign-in, no server. Daily challenges work on a plane.

How to play

Each board is split into irregular coloured regions called nooks. Place one cat in every row, every column and every nook — and never let two cats touch, not even diagonally.

  • Tap a cell to cross it out.
  • Double tap to place a cat.
  • Hold to mark a cell as a maybe.

You have three hearts. A wrong placement costs one and leaves a cross behind, so a mistake still teaches you something.
